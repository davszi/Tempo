## Glossary

- **Log-loss, Brier**: compare the predicted pass chance with pass (1) or fail (0). Lower is better.
- **RMSE(bins)**: calibration gap. Predictions are grouped in 20 bins; root mean square gap between each bin's mean prediction and its real pass rate, weighted by bin size. Multiply by 100 for percentage points (pp).
- **AUC**: chance that a random failed review got a lower prediction than a random passed one. 0.5 = coin flip, 1 = perfect.
- **RMSE / MAE vs truth**: gap between the prediction and the hidden true chance of a clean session (`p_clean`). Multiply by 100 for pp.
- **Days**: after each session, predicted days until the pass chance drops to 90% minus the true days. Negative = review scheduled too early. Relative error = error / true days. *Beyond 180 d*: sessions whose true time lies past the simulated horizon.
