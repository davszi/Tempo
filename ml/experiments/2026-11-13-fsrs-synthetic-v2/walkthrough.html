Step six

Training halves the error.

On the 60 test players, how far is each predicted chance from the true chance?

pp = percentage points: if the true chance is 70% and the model says 84%, it is 14 pp off. Constant 58% = a "model" that learns nothing and always says 58%, the share of sessions that pass in training. Any useful model must beat it.

Step seven

The answer key
barely helps.

B2, trained on the truth, is only 0.3 points closer than A2. Pass/fail was never the problem.

Where the last 14 points come from

One set of FSRS dials for everyone treats a line as one card. These players remember move by move, each with their own forgetting speed. No label can fix that mismatch.

What it means

Collecting richer signals than pass/fail would not close the gap. A model closer to how players forget, or per-player dials, might.

Conclusion

Against the old
300-player dials.

The previous run (v1) trained its dials on the old catalog, short lines included. We took those dials unchanged and scored them on the same 60 new test players as A2 and B2.

Defaultsv1, old dialsA2 pass/failB2 true chance
Close race

v1 guesses a bit closer

The old dials are 1 point closer to the true chance (13.1 vs 14.1 pp) and slightly better at spotting which line you'll forget. The catalog change did not break them.

A2 wins

A2 is more honest

When A2 says 70%, it is closer to 70% (gap 2.6 vs 4.1 points), and it schedules slightly less early overall (−0.14 vs −0.17 days).

Found a flaw

A2 stopped too soon

Even on A2's own training players, the v1 dials score better on A2's goal (0.553 vs 0.560). The optimizer started at the defaults and stopped at a worse spot. It didn't learn less; it got stuck.

Bottom line: new and old dials are about equally good, within 1 point. The cheap next step is to rerun A2 starting from the v1 dials and keep whichever is better on validation players. That would be a new, recorded attempt; it was not run.

Before you push

What to know.

  • ◦B2 stopped at the 300-step limitThe optimizer had not met its convergence test. The plan treats a higher limit as a new attempt, so it was not changed after seeing results.
  • ◦Families are now picked at randomThe backend catalog has no popularity column, so v1's own reported numbers are not comparable with v2's. The conclusion therefore scores the v1 dials on the new players instead.
  • ◦Dials compensate each otherA2 and B2 differ a lot in w8, w10 and w12 yet predict almost the same. Four dials sit on their limits.
  • ◦The 1000-player runs are not doneA1, A1c and B1 are still open in the plan. Nothing is committed or pushed yet.
